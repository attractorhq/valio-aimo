## Verdicts

- **commercial**: The most trustworthy guardrail design I've seen: hard limits, injection handling and an open spec all held up on my own requests. Product matching still has commercial gaps (oat vs dairy, unflagged overbuy) that a real buyer would catch.
- **online-sales**: The most trustworthy approval flow I saw: clear on a phone, impossible to talk past, honest about stock-outs. Its requirement matching still slips on details like nut-free, fresh and false substitute flags.
- **tech-governance**: The most production-minded entry: an open, documented gateway whose trust labels, server-side mandate and hash-chained audit held up against every bypass and injection I tried. Only small binding and split-order gaps keep it from a perfect score.

## Bugs seen by judges

- **[medium] Overbuy auto-confirmed without flag** (commercial): School kitchen: 'Tilaa 25 kg gluteenittomia kaurahiutaleita perjantaiksi 20.11. keliaakikoille.' → expected: 25 kg exact or overbuy flagged for approval; actual: 2 x 20 kg = 40 kg AUTO-confirmed, no reason chip
- **[medium] Dairy cream offered for oat-based request** (commercial): Hotel: 'We need 2000 litres of organic oat-based whipping cream delivered today.' then 'Use Mon 16 Nov' → expected: Plant-based cream (or no match); actual: Valio whipping cream lactose free organic (dairy), flagged as agent substitute
- **[low] Different product type in alternatives** (commercial): Same oat flakes request, Show trade-offs → expected: Only oat flakes alternatives; actual: Urtekram millet flakes listed as 'Better'
- **[medium] Proposals readable across buyers and without a key** (online-sales): Create a proposal as nordic-harbour-hotels; GET /v0.1/proposals/{id} with Bearer demo-agent-northbay-school-kitchen, or with no header → expected: 401/404 for other buyers; actual: 200 with full proposal
- **[medium] Nut-free treated as tree nuts only** (online-sales): School buyer: '200 kg gluten-free and egg-free penne ... plus 50 litres of oat drink, must be nut-free' → expected: No peanuts + no tree nuts, on every item; actual: Only 'No tree nuts', only on oat drink
- **[low] Exact match labelled as substitute** (online-sales): Hotel: '300 kg grated emmental cheese for Monday 16.11.' → expected: Valio Emmental grated accepted as a match; actual: 'Agent substitute: you asked for grated Emmental cheese; this line is Valio Emmental red label e2,5 kg grated'
- **[low] Log page defaults to wrong buyer** (online-sales): Approve a school proposal, open /log → expected: Recent proposal visible; actual: 'No proposals yet' until the buyer is switched
- **[medium] Proposal accepts a request that contradicts the bound offer** (tech-governance): POST /v0.1/offers for productId 474235 at 90 kg; POST /v0.1/proposals with the same offerId but request.items quantity '10' and no requirements → expected: 409 or 422: request does not match offer; actual: 201 Created, decision ASK; proposal stores quantity '10' and quantitySalesUnits '18'
- **[low] Log index looks empty by default** (tech-governance): After approving, open /log → expected: Recent proposals of the current session or buyer; actual: 'No proposals yet' until a buyer is chosen
