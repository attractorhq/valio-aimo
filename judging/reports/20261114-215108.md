## Verdicts

- **commercial**: The most trustworthy approval and audit design I have seen, with a real open interface, but it auto-bought soy yogurt for a school that asked for oat, and that is exactly the mistake a customer would never forgive.
- **online-sales**: The most trustworthy agent gateway I saw: Finnish requests, allergen-strict substitutes, stock-outs and injection attempts all handled honestly with a phone-ready approver view and a real open API. Vague requests and early-delivery trade-offs are the only soft spots.
- **tech-governance**: Best-in-class trust labels, server-side guardrails and audit chain I'd happily run in production, but the gateway let the agent auto-order grated cabbage for a cheese request, so the core safety promise has a hole.
