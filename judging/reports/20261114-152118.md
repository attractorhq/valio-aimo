## Verdicts

- **commercial**: Best-in-class guardrails, provenance and open API that I would trust with spend limits, but it auto-ordered sliced cheese for a grated request, so its product matching isn't yet good enough for a real buyer's budget.
- **online-sales**: The most trustworthy approval and guardrail design I've seen: a busy approver can say yes on a phone in seconds. But loose Finnish requests still get silent guesses and one confusing no-order screen.
- **tech-governance**: The most governance-mature entry: server-side AUTO/ASK/BLOCK, published trust labels with provenance, a verifiable audit chain and no way for an agent to approve. Points off for a 500 on mismatched requirements, untracked stock labelled DECLARED and an unauthenticated human channel.
