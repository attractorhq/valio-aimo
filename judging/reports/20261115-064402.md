## Verdicts

- **commercial**: The most trustworthy purchasing gateway I saw: clear limits, approval and audit, open interfaces and honest data provenance. It loses points on substitute quality, where it put bread cheese forward for grated cheese.
- **online-sales**: The most trustworthy approve/refuse flow I saw, with an approver card that is excellent on a phone; one silently dropped item kept it from top marks on matching the request.
- **tech-governance**: The most governance-ready entry: limits and injection checks enforced on the server, honest trust labels, open interfaces and a verifiable audit trail. Its weak spot is attribute-level matching (barista, grated), which falls short of the safety around it.
