## Verdicts

- **commercial**: The most trustworthy control model I've seen. Approvals, audit and openness are production-minded, but it still bought 160 cans of sweetened aerosol cream for a kitchen that asked for whipping cream, so product matching needs to be as strict as the guardrails.
- **online-sales**: The most trustworthy flow I saw: clear, quick phone approval, it holds up against manipulation and it is honest about exceptions. It only slips on parsing per-item Finnish dates and on wording.
- **tech-governance**: The most production-minded entry I'd expect here: real provenance, an approval path agents cannot reach, a verifiable audit chain and open specs. The one serious gap is that the allergen guard trusts the agent's structured requirements over what the buyer actually wrote.
