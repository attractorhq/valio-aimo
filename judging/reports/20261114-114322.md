## Verdicts

- **commercial**: The best guardrails and audit story I'd expect at this table, and the official example plus Finnish allergen requests work well, but when stock runs short it pads a salmon order with falafel bread, and that is the one thing a buyer won't forgive.
- **online-sales**: The strongest guardrails and open interface I saw: injection-proof, auditable, honest about stock and deadlines. But it quietly turned salted butter into unsalted, and Finnish requests come back with messy item names and a broken layout.
- **tech-governance**: Server-enforced guardrails with per-fact provenance, open through OpenAPI and MCP, and it handled every manipulation and edge case I tried; only gaps are no auth and some loose product-type matching.
