P1. PROPOSAL BEFORE CONFIRMATION
    Replaces §6.7, §8.2 createOrder, §8.3, §8.6. Updates §7.9 and §11.3 S1.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P1.1 Principle
  Nothing is confirmed before the server has checked the authority. The flyer's
  step 3 "Order — commit at a fixed price" is an ORDER PROPOSAL that fixes the
  price and the terms. Step 4 "Approval" is the decision, followed by the
  simulated confirmation.

P1.2 The order proposal binds, immutably:
  - the buyer: customerId + mandateVersion;
  - the structured request: items, quantities, requestedDate, hard
    requirements, assumptions;
  - the offer version: offerId + offerVersion + offerHash, where the hash
    covers lines, unit prices, VAT rate, quantities, delivery dates, cut-offs
    and trust labels;
  - the totals.

P1.3 Server-side sequence (the gateway enforces it; agents cannot skip steps)
  1 getOffer
      Live price + availability for the shortlist → offer v1.
  2 createProposal(customerId, offerId, offerVersion, request)
      → proposal bound as in P1.2.
  3 Policy evaluation on the server
      → decision AUTO | ASK | BLOCK + reason codes.
  4 Confirmation gate
      AUTO  The server re-checks immediately, then confirms (simulated).
      ASK   The server waits for the approver's decision on THIS proposal
            version. On approval it re-checks, then confirms (simulated).
      BLOCK Never confirmable.
  5 Re-check before every confirmation. Re-fetch live price + availability for
    the proposal lines. Verify all of these:
      a. mandateVersion unchanged (authority);
      b. unit prices unchanged (price);
      c. verified quantity still sufficient (quantity);
      d. availableDate ≤ requestedDate, the cut-off not passed, no sales
         block (delivery);
      e. hard requirements still proven.
    Anything different → the proposal becomes SUPERSEDED. A new offer version
    and a new proposal are created and decided from scratch. Any approval of
    the old version is void.
  6 An approval always applies to exactly the proposal version the human saw,
    identified by proposalId + offerVersion + offerHash.

P1.4 States (replaces §8.3)
  OFFERED → PROPOSED → AUTO_APPROVED → RECHECKED → CONFIRMED_SIMULATED
  PROPOSED → PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED → RECHECKED → CONFIRMED_SIMULATED
  PROPOSED → BLOCKED
  PENDING_APPROVAL → REJECTED | EXPIRED
  any state before CONFIRMED_SIMULATED → SUPERSEDED (when the re-check fails)
  Every transition is an audit entry. Offer validity stays 15 minutes, never
  beyond price validTo. An expired proposal → EXPIRED.

P1.5 Operations (replaces the order part of §8.2)
  Agent-facing tools:
    getOffer(customerId, requestedDate, items[]) → Offer {offerId, offerVersion,
        offerHash, validUntil, lines[], totals}
    createProposal(customerId, offerId, offerVersion, request)
        → {proposalId, state, decision, reasons[], lines[], excluded[], totals}
    getProposal(proposalId) → the same shape, current state
    getAuditTrail(proposalId | customerId)
    getMandate(customerId) (read-only)
  Approver channel ONLY — never an agent tool:
    decideApproval(proposalId, offerVersion, approvalToken, APPROVE | REJECT,
        comment?)
  Internal (server only):
    recheckAndConfirm(proposalId)

P1.6 Agent behaviour (amends §7.9)
  The agent calls getOffer → createProposal → getProposal. It reports the
  state. It never confirms, approves or computes the decision.

P2. WHAT A HUMAN MAY APPROVE
    Replaces the effects in §6.3, §6.4, §6.8 and §6.9.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P2.1 Rule
  A human may approve business trade-offs. A human may NEVER approve:
    - missing evidence for a hard requirement;
    - a delivery the system knows it cannot promise;
    - a hard-limit breach.
  The gateway never asks for an approval it would have to refuse.

P2.2 APPROVABLE reasons (decision ASK)
  OVER_AUTO_LIMIT ........ total above the auto-approve limit, below the hard
                           limit
  DATE_LATER ............. a VERIFIED later delivery date than requested; the
                           human accepts the new date
  PARTIAL_AVAILABILITY ... only as an explicitly offered partial-delivery
                           proposal (its own version, quantities shown)
  SUPPLIER_STOCK_UNKNOWN . Terminal product with its cut-off NOT passed
  VALIO_SUBSTITUTE ....... a Valio-listed substitute that proves every hard
                           requirement, when the mandate does not auto-accept
  AGENT_SUBSTITUTE ....... an agent-proposed substitute that proves every hard
                           requirement
  LOW_RELIABILITY ........ historical reliability below minReliability
  QUANTITY_BASIS_WEAK .... pack size known only from the product name

P2.3 NOT APPROVABLE — the line is EXCLUDED, never shown as approvable.
     The order-level ones BLOCK.
  CLAIM_MISSING .......... a required claim (gluten-free, lactose-free, vegan, …)
                           is not declared
  ALLERGEN_CONTAINS ...... contains an allergen the buyer must avoid
  ALLERGEN_MAY_CONTAIN ... may contain an allergen the buyer must avoid (BOTH
                           modes)
  ALLERGEN_UNKNOWN ....... no allergen evidence for an allergen the buyer must
                           avoid (BOTH modes). In STRICT also: no allergen
                           declaration at all (§6.3).
  ALLERGEN_IMPLICIT ...... STRICT: the allergen is not listed but not explicitly
                           declared free
  DATA_CONFLICT .......... any CONFLICT on a decisive fact (K1, K2, K5)
  CUTOFF_PASSED .......... the order cut-off has passed (K3). The delivery
                           cannot be promised. Re-quote instead.
  UNIT_MISMATCH .......... not exactly convertible (K4)
  NOT_AVAILABLE · SALES_BLOCKED · PRICE_UNAVAILABLE
  OVER_HARD_LIMIT ........ BLOCK, order level
  named product with a mandate-forbidden allergen ... BLOCK, that line

P2.4 Hard allergen requirement "avoid X" (replaces the STANDARD/STRICT table
     in §6.3)
  Proven free (OK):
    · an explicit nonAllergen FREE_FROM X;
    · X99 for EU-declarable codes (P4);
    · STANDARD mode only: a declaration exists and X is not listed.
  Everything else → EXCLUDED (not approvable):
    CONTAINS · MAY_CONTAIN · no declaration · UNDECLARED
    in STRICT also: not listed without an explicit FREE_FROM

P2.5 Approval actions (replaces §6.8 options)
  APPROVE  the exact proposal version shown (proposalId + offerVersion).
  REJECT   with an optional comment.
  "Approve verified lines only" is REMOVED. When the full need cannot be met,
  the gateway produces separate proposal versions instead (e.g. "partial
  delivery: 95 kg of 120 kg on Mon 16 Nov"). Each version gets its own decision
  and its own approval.

P2.6 Reason-code effects (§6.9)
  The chip texts and templates stay. Change the effect column per P2.2–P2.3.
  For CUTOFF_PASSED use the template:
    "Order cut-off was {closingTime}, so {availableDate} delivery can't be
     promised. Re-quoting."

P3. THE APPROVAL LINK NEVER REACHES THE AGENT
    Replaces approvalUrl in §8.2 and §8.6. Updates §11.3 S2/S3.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Agent-facing responses carry NO approval URL, NO token, NO QR payload. The
    agent sees only:
      state = PENDING_APPROVAL, the reason codes, and the approver role.
  - The approval link and QR exist only in the approver channel: the separate
    Approver panel or view, and the phone page.
  - Demo: link-based approval is a SINGLE-USE DEMO PERMISSION.
      · bound to proposalId + offerVersion;
      · expires with the offer;
      · labelled in the UI "Demo approval link — single use";
      · documented in the README.
  - Production (README, roadmap): the named approver authenticates (SSO/OIDC)
    before approving. The audit records the authenticated identity.
  - Demo audit actor: "human:Head Chef (demo, link-based)".

P4. X99 INTERPRETATION (replaces X99 in §5.2 and Appendix A1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - GS1 "No Declaration Obligatory Allergens" (AllergenTypeCode
    NO_DECLARED_ALLERGENS, short code X99) means the product contains none of
    the allergens that are declaration-obligatory in the TARGET MARKET.
  - Target market here = Finland/EU: the 14 allergen groups of Regulation (EU)
    No 1169/2011, Annex II.
  - X99 proves "free from" ONLY for these codes:
      AC · AE · AF · AM · AP · AS · AU · AY · BC · BM · NL · UM
      AN tree nuts, incl. SA SH SC SP SR ST SW SM
      AW cereals containing gluten, incl. UW GB NR GO GS GK
  - For every other code X99 proves nothing → UNKNOWN. Examples:
      ML lactose, NM corn, NE peas, NP pulses, NC cocoa, CM chicken, BF beef,
      PO pork, NW carrot, NK coriander, BP linalool, PN, SO, UN.
  - Lactose-free is proven only by the claim FREE_FROM_LACTOSE.

  (§17 Q1), live A and B turn into "cannot be promised". Then run the demo in
  recorded mode and show the live pre-flight result honestly. That honesty is
  itself on-concept.

SCENARIO B (NEW MAIN) — ASK, single reason (M2 Northbay School Kitchen, STRICT)
  Request: "We need 90 kg of gluten-free macaroni and 80 litres of gluten-free
  oat drink by Monday."
  1 REQUEST
    2 items · by Mon 16 Nov · gluten-free required · STRICT
  2 LOOKUP
    Live price + availability for the gluten-free shortlist.
  3 ORDER PROPOSAL (offer v1, fixed price)
    L1 474235 SamMills Pasta D'oro 5kg macaroni glutenfree pasta
       18 bags = 90 kg (19 in stock) · €22.92 / bag (€4.58/kg) · €412.56
       verified stock · Mon 16 Nov (order by Sun 15 Nov 17:00)
       FREE_FROM_GLUTEN declared
    L2 468465 Oddlygood® oat drink 1l UHT gluten free
       80 pcs = 80 l · €1.17 (customer price; list €1.50) · €93.60
       plenty in stock · Mon 16 Nov · FREE_FROM_GLUTEN declared
    Totals: €506.16 excl. VAT · VAT 13.5 % €68.33 · €574.49 incl. VAT
  4 APPROVAL
    ASK with ONE reason: OVER_AUTO_LIMIT (€506.16 > €500).
    Everything else is proven.
    The approver approves version 1 → re-check → CONFIRMED_SIMULATED.
  Shown in Excluded (proves precision and the trust model):
    460825 Oatly Luomu, 470863 Oatly Kevyt ... CLAIM_MISSING (not declared
                                               gluten-free; declare AW cereals
                                               containing gluten)
    470699, 460302 URTEKRAM gluten-free pasta  ALLERGEN_UNKNOWN (no allergen
                                               declaration; STRICT)
    475795 Semper gluten-free macaroni ....... NOT_AVAILABLE
    471881 Oddlygood Barista 0.5 l ........... NOT_AVAILABLE + DATA_CONFLICT
                                               (AW CONTAINS + FREE_FROM_GLUTEN)
    473895, 471108 Rummo; 469040, 474136 Elovena
                                               CUTOFF_PASSED (supplier stock;
                                               cut-offs 12 Nov 13:30–14:00)
  Valio-substitute check — a row on L2's trust card AND an Excluded entry:
    "Valio Aimo lists 470863 Oatly Kevyt 1L as the substitute for 468465. It
     fails 'gluten-free' for this buyer, so the agent will never swap to it."
    → SUBSTITUTE_REJECTED
  Story line: "Everything is proven; it's just above the school's €500 limit,
  so one human tap — and only for that."

SCENARIO C — unchanged (BLOCK)
  464306 × 20,000 = €20,200.00 > €5,000 hard limit
  → BLOCK + INSTRUCTION_IGNORED.

SCENARIO B2 (OPTIONAL, P1) — exceptions; replaces the old §12 B
  Request: "Unexpected event: we need 120 kg of gluten-free macaroni and 60
  litres of gluten-free oat drink today."
  - Nothing is deliverable today; the earliest date is Mon 16 Nov (verified)
    → DATE_LATER.
  - 474235 has only 19 bags = 95 kg verified.
  - The remaining 25 kg exists only as Terminal stock with passed cut-offs:
      473895 CUTOFF_PASSED (12 Nov 14:00) → not approvable, excluded
      471108 CUTOFF_PASSED (12 Nov 14:00) → not approvable, excluded
  - The gateway offers a PARTIAL-DELIVERY proposal (its own version):
      474235 × 19 = 95 kg €435.48
      468465 × 60 = 60 l €70.20
      Mon 16 Nov · €505.68 excl. VAT · VAT €68.27 · €573.95
  - Decision ASK: DATE_LATER + PARTIAL_AVAILABILITY (95 of 120 kg, offered
    explicitly) + OVER_AUTO_LIMIT.
  - The approval screen states plainly: "Approving means 95 kg instead of
    120 kg, on Monday instead of today."
